Kindle listing optimisation for authors

Your listing has a short window to earn a click. I review your cover, title area and description, then rewrite the description so the premise, the stakes and the genre are clear at a glance.

Request a quoteView my work

What is included

  • A review of your current Amazon listing: cover, title, description and first impression
  • A rewritten book description with a clear hook, setup and emotional pull
  • Notes on how the cover signals genre and mood at thumbnail size
  • The final description in a file you can paste into Kindle Direct Publishing

What I check before delivery

  • The hook sits in the first two lines, before the “Read more” fold
  • Genre, setting and tone are visible without spoiling the plot
  • Names, series details and facts match your book
  • Formatting works in the KDP description box

What I need from you

  • Your Amazon link, or your current description and cover
  • Genre, series position and comparable books
  • Anything the description must keep or avoid
  • Your launch or promotion date, if any

My process

1

Send your listing and a short note about the book

2

I review it and tell you what I would change and why

3

I draft the new description and cover notes

4

You review; I revise once with your feedback

5

You get the final copy, ready to paste

Frequently asked

Do you rewrite the whole description?

Yes. I draft a new description in your book’s tone and keep the facts true to your story.

Can you change my cover?

I give notes on how the cover reads on a listing. If a cover redesign is needed, we can talk about that separately.

Will this raise my sales?

A clearer listing helps readers understand the book. I cannot promise sales, because rankings and sales also depend on reviews, price, promotion and timing.
