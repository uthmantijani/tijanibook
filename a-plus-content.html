Amazon A+ Content design for authors

Amazon A+ Content gives your product page extra space below the description. I design that space as a set of visual modules that extend your cover’s mood and show readers what the book offers.

Request a quoteView my work

What is included

  • A four-part visual layout built around your cover, genre and series
  • Reader-facing copy for each module, such as a key line, tropes and book formats
  • Consistent type, colour and imagery taken from your cover
  • Files sized for the Amazon A+ Content editor

What I check before delivery

  • Text is readable on a phone
  • Images are licensed, supplied by you or created for the project
  • Spelling, series order and book details are correct
  • Modules follow Amazon’s A+ Content guidelines as I know them

What I need from you

  • Your cover file and series details
  • The tropes and key lines you want readers to see
  • Any images you have the right to use
  • Your Amazon link, so I can see the current page

My process

Frequently asked

Do I need an Amazon account that supports A+ Content?

A+ Content is added through your Amazon KDP account. I design the modules; you upload them, and I can tell you what to look for.

Can you use my own images?

Yes, if you have the right to use them. I will not add images that are not licensed for this use.

Do you show results?

The case study shows the design and how the page is laid out. It does not claim sales or conversion results.
