Amazon categories and keywords for authors

A book placed in categories that match its story is easier for the right readers to find while they browse. I research your categories and keywords and set them up to describe your book clearly.

Request a quoteView my work

What is included

  • A review of the categories your book sits in now
  • A shortlist of better-fitting categories and the reason for each
  • Keyword phrases for the seven KDP keyword boxes
  • A short note on how to apply the changes in KDP

What I check before delivery

  • Categories match the story, not just the genre label
  • Keywords are phrases a reader would search for
  • No misleading or restricted terms
  • Your KDP choices are consistent with your description

What I need from you

  • Your Amazon link or KDP bookshelf details
  • Genre, tropes and comparable authors
  • Your current categories and keywords
  • Your target readers

My process

Frequently asked

Can you guarantee a ranking?

No. The case study shows a rank change between two screenshots, but rankings also move with sales, reviews and Amazon’s own system.

Do you change my categories for me?

Categories and keywords are set in your KDP account, so you apply them. I give you the exact choices and how to enter them.
