Why good books get passed over

Readers decide on the product page in seconds. These are the problems I see most.

1

The hook is buried

The description starts with background, not with the reason to read.

2

The cover and copy disagree

A dark cover with a light description, or the other way round, confuses the promise.

3

The page stops at the description

A+ Content space is empty, so readers get no sense of tropes, series or mood.

4

Wrong categories

A book placed away from its real story is harder to find while readers browse.

Before and after a listing review

Before

  • A summary that explains the plot in order
  • The main character and goal appear late
  • Cover mood and description tone do not match
  • Product page has no designed extra content

After

  • A hook in the first two lines
  • Character, goal and stakes in a clear setup
  • Genre and tone visible, plot not given away
  • A+ Content and categories that support the same promise

Ask a question
What do I send you to start?

Your Amazon link (or your current description and cover), your genre, and what you want to change. The form below asks for the same details.

Do you work with authors outside the United States?

Yes. I work with authors anywhere, and all the work is done online.

Will you promise more sales or a higher ranking?

No. Sales and rankings depend on many things, including reviews, price, promotion and timing. My case studies show what I changed and what the screenshots showed, and say so when results could have other causes.

Are the case studies shown as client results?

Some are projects done on real listings, and some are sample refinements that I wrote from a public listing. Each card says which it is.
